HOW A JOB RUNS

From breakdown call to running line

  • 1

    Call comes in

    Tell us the make, model and fault symptoms. We can be on the phone or on-site at short notice.

  • 2

    Bench diagnosis

    The drive or card is tested under load and traced down to the failed component or stage.

  • 3

    Repair & test

    Component-level repair, then a full functional test before anything goes back to your line.

  • 4

    Handover & follow-up

    Unit returned or reinstalled on-site, with ongoing maintenance support if you need it.
